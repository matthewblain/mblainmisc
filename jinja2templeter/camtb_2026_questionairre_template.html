The California Mountain Biking Coalition 2026 Legislative Recreation Questionnaire is one part of CAMTB's effort to inform voters in the 2026 General Election. This is a neutral guide: it is a response to a questionnaire posed to all candidates in the 2026 Legislature. E.g. all Assembly Members and half of the Senators (those with odd numbered seats).

We are also endorsing select candidates and ballot issues. See our CAMTB Endorsements Posts to learn more.

The responses from the Legislative Questionnaire are provided verbatim below. We welcome responses from candidates who have not yet responded.
